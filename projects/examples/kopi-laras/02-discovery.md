# Discovery Summary: Kopi Laras (Fictional)

**Version:** 1.1 | **Date:** 2026-08-14 | **Sources:** interview 2026-08-11, questionnaire, asset review 2026-08-10

## 1. Background and business model
- [Client] Roasts beans from three partner farms around Malang. Sells roasted beans in 250g retail bags and 1kg wholesale bags.
- [Client] Wholesale to cafes is the main revenue line. Retail is sold at the roastery and by WhatsApp.

## 2. Products (priority order)
1. Wholesale 1kg single-origin beans (three origins)
2. House blend for espresso, wholesale
3. Retail 250g bags

## 3. Audience assumption cards
**Segment: Cafe owner or head barista outside Malang**
- Situation: [Client] Runs a small cafe in East Java, buys 5-30kg per month.
- Goal: stable supply with consistent taste.
- Trigger: current supplier runs out or changes quality.
- Main question before acting: "Will you have this origin next month, and can you invoice my business?"
- Barrier: [Hypothesis] Distrust of small roasters' consistency.
- Evidence: owner's account of the last 20 wholesale conversations (unverified count).
- How we will verify: review 10 recent WhatsApp inquiries with the owner.

**Segment: Home brewer** (secondary). Goal: buy a 250g bag. Served by retail info, not the focus.
