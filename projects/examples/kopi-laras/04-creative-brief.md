# Creative Brief: Kopi Laras (Fictional)

## Direction A: Field Ledger (approved)

1. **Strategic rationale:** make supply reliability visible, the core of the positioning.
2. **Audience insight:** buyers plan menus around predictable beans.
3. **Visual concept:** A roaster's working ledger. Each origin reads like a dated, measured entry with clear status.
4. **Typography:** Fraunces (SIL Open Font License) for headings, giving a printed, editorial voice. Source Sans 3 (OFL) for body. IBM Plex Mono (OFL) for dates, weights, and stock only. Scale 16/20/26/36/56.
5. **Color:** ink #1d1b19, paper #f6f1ea, clay #a4522e for actions only, leaf #3f5a3c for "in stock", amber #8a5a00 for "low stock". Proportion roughly 75 paper / 20 ink / 5 accents. Reason: paper and ink support the ledger concept; clay comes from the existing logo.
6. **Layout:** 12-column grid, left-aligned. Thin rule lines separate entries. Stock tables are real tables.
7. **Images:** farm and harvest photos at moderate size, never full-bleed hero. Type and data lead.
8. **Motion:** none except 150ms button feedback and form state changes.
9. **Do:** show dates; show units; use tables for comparison; keep clay for actions; name farms. **Don't:** full-bleed lifestyle hero; gradients; cards for every item; vague adjectives; emoji.
10. **References:** a printed seed catalog (principle: dated, structured entries); a train timetable (principle: scannable status). Neither is copied.

**Axes that differ from B:** density (higher), image role (data-led), typographic voice (editorial + mono).
**Anti-slop concept check:** A1 Pass, A2 Pass (competitors could not adopt it without publishing stock), A3 Pass.

## Direction B: Warm Ritual (not chosen)
Photo-led, warm palette, slow scroll storytelling of the brewing ritual. Not chosen: it matched competitor conventions and did not answer the supply question.

## Decision
Approved: Field Ledger | Owner, 2026-08-28 | Note: owner asked to keep the existing clay color from the logo.
