# Brand Strategy: Kopi Laras (Fictional)

## 1. Positioning statement
For cafe owners in East Java
who need the same coffee every month,
Kopi Laras is the roaster
that publishes each origin's harvest, roast date, and stock,
because it works with three named farms on fixed seasonal contracts [Client].
Unlike lifestyle roasters, we sell reliability you can check before you order.

## 2. Audience insight
[Client] Cafe owners lose regulars when a bean changes without notice. They value predictability over novelty.

## 3. Personality
| Trait | Is | Is not | Sounds like (EN) | Sounds like (ID) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Precise | Dates, weights, terms | Cold or technical jargon | "Roasted 2 Oct. 18kg left." | "Disangrai 2 Okt. Sisa 18kg." |
| Grounded | Names farms and people | Romantic origin myths | "Grown by the Sumber Wangi group." | "Ditanam kelompok tani Sumber Wangi." |
| Helpful | Answers MOQ and invoices upfront | Salesy | "Minimum order 5kg. We issue invoices." | "Minimal pesan 5kg. Kami menerbitkan invoice." |

The farm group name is fictional.

## 4. Messaging hierarchy
- Primary: "Coffee you can plan your menu around."
- Supporting: named farms (proof: farm pages); published roast dates (proof: stock table); clear wholesale terms (proof: terms table).

## 5. Differentiation
| Competitor convention | Keep or break | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Full-bleed lifestyle hero | Break | Does not answer the buyer's supply question |
| Tasting notes per origin | Keep | Buyers expect them |
| "Contact us for wholesale" with no terms | Break | Terms are the main pre-purchase question |

## 6. Scope
Logo kept (existing). New: type system, color roles, tone of voice, web application. Illustration and packaging out of scope.
