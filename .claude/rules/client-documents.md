# Client-facing document rules

- Provide English and Indonesian versions when the document is sent to clients.
- English: clear international English, no idioms. Indonesian: natural, professional, formal "Anda".
- Always state timezones (WIB, UTC+7 by default for Indonesian clients) and currency fields as placeholders.
- These are templates, not legal advice. Do not add jurisdiction-specific legal claims.
- Every deliverable listed needs an acceptance criterion.
