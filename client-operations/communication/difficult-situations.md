# Difficult Situations

**Purpose:** Handle common friction early and in writing, with the same approach every time.

## Scope creep

**Signal:** A request adds pages, features, integrations, or changes an approved decision.
**Action:** Acknowledge, classify against the SOW, offer options.

EN:
> Thanks, this makes sense for {{GOAL}}. It sits outside the current scope (SOW section {{N}}). We can (a) add it through a change request: estimate {{EFFORT}}, {{COST}}, timeline impact {{DAYS}}; (b) swap it for {{EXISTING_ITEM}} at no extra cost; or (c) schedule it for phase 2. Which works best?

ID:
> Terima kasih, permintaan ini relevan untuk {{TUJUAN}}. Namun item ini di luar lingkup saat ini (SOW bagian {{N}}). Pilihannya: (a) kami tambahkan melalui permintaan perubahan: estimasi {{UPAYA}}, {{BIAYA}}, dampak jadwal {{HARI}}; (b) menggantikan {{ITEM_LAIN}} tanpa biaya tambahan; atau (c) dijadwalkan pada fase 2. Mana yang paling sesuai?

## Approval delay

**Action:** Remind once with the impact, then adjust the plan in writing.

EN:
> We are waiting for approval of {{DELIVERABLE}}, sent {{DATE}}. Without it by {{DATE}}, launch moves from {{OLD}} to {{NEW}}. Reply "approved" or send consolidated feedback.

ID:
> Kami masih menunggu persetujuan {{HASIL_KERJA}} yang dikirim {{TANGGAL}}. Jika belum diterima hingga {{TANGGAL}}, peluncuran bergeser dari {{LAMA}} ke {{BARU}}. Mohon balas "disetujui" atau kirim masukan terkonsolidasi.

## Missing content

**Action:** Name the missing items, offer a fallback, state the deadline.
- Offer: we build with clearly marked placeholders and the client approves that pages will not launch with placeholders, or the client purchases copywriting as a change request.
- Never ship invented copy, testimonials, or statistics as a workaround.

## Additional revisions

**Action:** Show the round count, then offer a change request.
> EN: This is round {{N}} of {{INCLUDED}}. We can continue under a change request at {{RATE}} per round.
> ID: Ini putaran ke-{{N}} dari {{TERMASUK}}. Revisi berikutnya dapat dilanjutkan melalui permintaan perubahan dengan tarif {{TARIF}} per putaran.

## Unclear acceptance criteria

**Action:** Before delivery, propose testable criteria and ask for written agreement.
> EN: To confirm "done" for {{DELIVERABLE}}, we propose: {{CRITERIA_LIST}}. Please confirm or edit by {{DATE}}.
> ID: Agar "selesai" untuk {{HASIL_KERJA}} jelas, kami usulkan kriteria berikut: {{DAFTAR_KRITERIA}}. Mohon konfirmasi atau koreksi paling lambat {{TANGGAL}}.

## Feedback from multiple stakeholders that conflicts

Ask the named approver in the SOW to consolidate. Do not resolve conflicts between client stakeholders yourself.
