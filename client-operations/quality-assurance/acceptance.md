# Acceptance / Penerimaan Hasil Kerja

**Purpose:** Close each deliverable with written, criteria-based acceptance.

## Process

1. Studio delivers with: link or files, the acceptance criteria from the SOW, the QA report, and known issues.
2. Client tests within {{N}} working days (UAT). Client reports issues in one list with steps to reproduce and screenshots.
3. Studio classifies each item: **Defect** (does not meet criteria, fixed in scope) or **Change** (new requirement, goes to change request).
4. Client signs acceptance when criteria are met. Low-severity defects may be accepted with a fix date.

## Common mistakes

- Accepting by chat message "ok looks good". Confirm in the form or by email quoting the criteria.
- Mixing new requests into the defect list.
