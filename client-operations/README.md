# Client Operations

**Purpose:** Client-facing and commercial documents for Indonesian and international clients.

**Important:** These are operational templates, not legal advice. Contracts, tax treatment, and data protection terms depend on jurisdiction. Have a qualified professional review the final contract for each client.

## Conventions

- Dates: ISO 8601 (`2026-10-12`) in internal docs. Client-facing docs may also spell out the date ("12 October 2026" / "12 Oktober 2026").
- Time: always state the timezone (`10:00 WIB (UTC+7)`, `09:00 CET (UTC+1)`).
- Currency: `{{CURRENCY}}` field. Show tax treatment explicitly (for example "excludes VAT/PPN").
- Placeholders: `{{UPPER_SNAKE_CASE}}`.
