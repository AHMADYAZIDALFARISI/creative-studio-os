# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone can take part in this project without harassment, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or language. Contributions in English or Bahasa Indonesia are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' ideas and sources.
- Protect privacy: never post client data, personal data, or private conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may block participants who break this code. Report problems privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
