# Audience Assumptions and User Journeys

**Purpose:** Describe who uses the product and what they try to do, without inventing personas.
**Use when:** Discovery step 4 and before information architecture.

## Audience assumption cards

We use assumption cards instead of fictional personas with stock photos and invented names. A card states what we believe and how we will check it.

```markdown
### Segment: {{SEGMENT_NAME}}
- Situation: [Client/Hypothesis] who they are, in one sentence
- Goal: what they want to get done
- Trigger: what makes them start looking
- Main question before acting:
- Barrier: what stops them
- Evidence: source, or "unverified"
- How we will verify: interview 5 users / analytics / client sales data
```

## Journey map

Map one journey per primary segment. Keep it to five to seven steps.

| Step | User action | User question | Touchpoint | Pain or risk | Design response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | Sees a post | "Is this for me?" | Instagram | Unclear offer | Single-line value statement |

## Quality check

- Each segment links to a business goal.
- Each journey step has a design response, or a note that no response is needed.
- Unverified assumptions appear in the risk log.

## Common mistakes

- Personas with ages, hobbies, and names that no data supports.
- Mapping the ideal journey only. Map where people fail.
