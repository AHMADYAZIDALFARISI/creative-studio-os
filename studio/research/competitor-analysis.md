# Competitor Analysis

**Purpose:** Understand the competitive context so the work differentiates instead of copying.
**Use when:** Discovery step 5.
**Inputs:** Three to five competitors named by the client, plus up to two found by us.
**Output:** One table and five to eight findings.

## Method

1. Visit each competitor's site on mobile and desktop. Record the date.
2. Fill the table. Write observations, not judgments.
3. Identify **category conventions**: patterns most competitors share.
4. Decide which conventions to keep (users expect them) and which to break (they create sameness).

## Rules

- Do not copy layouts, copy, or assets. Use competitors to find gaps.
