# Brand Positioning

**Purpose:** Define what the client stands for, for whom, and why it is different.
**Use when:** After discovery is confirmed and before creative direction.
**Inputs:** Discovery summary, competitor findings, audience cards.
**Output:** Positioning section of [templates/brand-strategy.md](../../templates/brand-strategy.md).

## Positioning statement

```
For {{TARGET_AUDIENCE}}
who {{NEED_OR_PROBLEM}},
{{BRAND}} is the {{CATEGORY}}
that {{KEY_BENEFIT}},
because {{REASON_TO_BELIEVE}}.
Unlike {{MAIN_ALTERNATIVE}}, we {{DIFFERENCE}}.
```

Each slot must be specific enough that a competitor could not sign it. "High quality and great service" fails this test.

## Personality and tone

Pick three to four traits. For each, define what it is and what it is not.

| Trait | Is | Is not | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct | States price and process upfront | Blunt or cold | "Delivery takes 3 working days." |

## Messaging hierarchy

1. Primary message: one sentence.
2. Three supporting messages, each with proof the client can show.
3. Proof points: only real facts. Write "needs proof" when none exists yet.

## Quality check

- Reason to believe is a verifiable fact.
- Tone traits include "is not" boundaries.
- The client can repeat the primary message without reading it.
