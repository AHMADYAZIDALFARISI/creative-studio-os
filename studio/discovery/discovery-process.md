# Discovery Process

**Purpose:** Turn a client inquiry into a confirmed understanding of goals, users, constraints, and success criteria.
**Use when:** After onboarding and before any proposal with fixed pricing.
**Inputs:** Intake form, client's existing assets, access to at least one decision-maker.
**Output:** A discovery summary built on [templates/client-discovery.md](../../templates/client-discovery.md).

## Evidence labels

Tag every statement in discovery and strategy documents with one label.

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **[Client]** | The client told us. Not checked. | "[Client] Most orders come from Instagram." |
| **[Verified]** | We checked it against a source we can cite. | "[Verified] The current site has no `lang` attribute (checked 2026-10-12)." |
| **[Hypothesis]** | We believe it, but it needs a test. | "[Hypothesis] Office buyers want invoice-ready checkout." |
| **[Recommendation]** | Our proposed action, with a reason. | "[Recommendation] Add a B2B order form because..." |

Never present a hypothesis as research. Never invent a market statistic. If a number matters and we lack a source, write "needs data" and name who can supply it.

## Steps

1. **Review assets (1-2 hours).** Website, social accounts, brand files, analytics access if granted. Log findings as [Verified] with dates.
2. **Run the stakeholder interview (60-90 minutes).** Use the [interview guide](interview-guide.md). Record with permission.
3. **Map the business.** Business model, products and services, revenue drivers, sales channels.
4. **Map the users.** Draft audience assumptions and journeys with [audience-and-journeys.md](../research/audience-and-journeys.md). Mark them [Hypothesis] until validated.
5. **Scan competitors.** Use [competitor-analysis.md](../research/competitor-analysis.md). Analyze three to five, not fifteen.
6. **List constraints.** Budget range, deadline, tech stack, hosting, content readiness, legal or regulatory needs, approval chain.
7. **Define success.** Two to five measurable criteria with a baseline or a plan to get one.
8. **Write risks and dependencies.** Each with an owner and a mitigation.
9. **Confirm with the client.** Send the summary. Ask them to correct facts. Their written confirmation closes the gate.

## Quality check

- Every goal has a measure. "Better brand awareness" fails. "Increase qualified inquiry form submissions from {{BASELINE}} per month" passes.
- At least one decision-maker confirmed the summary in writing.
- No unlabeled claims.
- Content readiness is explicit: who writes copy, who supplies photos, by when.

## Common mistakes

- Accepting the client's solution ("we need an app") as the requirement. Ask what problem the app solves.
- Skipping the approval chain question. Hidden approvers cause late rework.
- Treating one stakeholder's opinion as the company position.
